## Throwaway
### I.
The clock's tongue stills: no longer ticks but hisses.
He counts the cracks in walls where childhood hid,
Each splinter a ghost that hums his name,
A hymn he choked to dust. A world's a prism with no aid.
Fractured to shards; he drinks the glass, unblinking,
Swearing the pain is proof he's still functioning.

### II.
The mirrors now refuse his face, they wept.
Black oil instead of glass. His hands grow thin,
Threadbare as moth-winged lies. He licks the static
From outlets, swears the buzz is holy.
The sky bleeds static. Scars? Just wounds the dark
Infected. He laughs, a sound like rusted hinges.

### III.
The shadows speak now. Not with voices, but teeth.
They gnaw the edges of his breath, whispering:
"You're lighter when you bleed."
His veins, spooled,
Becomes a map of roads he will never walk.
He eats the moon's sorrowful dust, swears it's nourishment,
While his reflection drowns in a bowl of soup or a lack of trust.

### IV.
The walls have tongues. The floor? A revenge throat.
He dances with the cracks, their jagged waltz
A dirge for sense he can't name. "Is this real?"
He asks for the rot beneath his fingernails.
The answer? Static. A scream. A nothing.
His soul, a moth, now gnaws the void's black spine:
And the boy? Just ash, scattered by a wind that isn't fined.

## It's A Rewind
### I.
The world was a lullaby, soft and spun,
till he found the content of the crimsons.
It's pages whispered of blood and bone,
Of secrets that split a child's fragile throne.

### II.
He clutched his knees, a fortress of skin,
The seven year old in despair could not handle the pin,
As shadows gnawed through the light within.
The stars? Just rot in a velvet sky,
And laughter became a rusted cry.

### III.
Then came the whispers of bedsheets torn,
All in his head however since he had caretakers of his own,
Of lips that lied where trust was born.
His playfulness now stared with hollow eyes,
A circus of lies under fevered skin and skies.

### IV.
He tried to plug the cracks with glue,
But the poison seeped through every hue.
Fingers in ears, he sang lullabies,
Yet the threat of the world drowned his innocent cries'.

### V.
What if he'd never seeked for knowledge?
Could his heart still bloom, unscathed and unaged?
Would the roses of childhood still cling to his name,
Or had the storm already claimed his frame?

### VI.
Now he walks with a knot on his throat,
A tightrope strung with utter frost and hoax.
Each step a scream, each breath a ghost.
Line in front of him like withers while he's all alone.
The boy? A flicker in his perception led to void's cold gaze,
Surprisingly, he thought he was in the correct mind range.
A seed that bloomed too soon in the dark's embrace,
Turned bitter quickly to his own tame.

## What He Was
### I.
He catalogued ants by their stride's design,
Named each cloud a myth, mapped the moon's spine.
A boy who kissed dewdrops at dawn's first sigh,
Swore he'd unravel the sky before he could die.

### II.
His pockets held stones that whispered *why?*,
He climbed trees to pluck the sun's last cry.
The world was a riddle, his grin the key,
A clockwork of wonder, relentless and free.

### III.
Teachers called him a far instead of a star, but he still kept chasing the void,
Dug for fossils where all roots would be devoid.
"What's beyond the edge?" he'd ask, eyes alight,
Ignoring the shadows that clung to the night.

### IV.
He later wish he had stopped but currently he went and lend,
Then came the book with no title, no end,
Its pages a labyrinth he couldn't defend.
It sang of a door where the answers were sharp:
A hymn he hummed, though his heart grew too far.

### V.
Now he guards a key he can't place the lock for,
A seed in his palm that burns, yet he'll not drop.
Hence it extinguishes with pure lash,
*What if the stars could just be ash?*
He wondered as he fell and woke up half-awake,
And **the dewdrops he kissed were the tears of a wake?**

## Gave Up To Knowledge
### I.
The walls screamed: thick with the rot he'd swallowed,
Their teeth like truths that chewed his knees to gravel.
He sprinted, a ghost in a hallway that breathed,
Each step a bullet, the dark at his back wreathed in death.

### II.
The monsters wore faces he'd memorized:
His mother's laugh, his father's lies,
Teachers' hands that gouged the truth into his spine:
"Run, little sage!" they hissed, "This is where you'll drown in thine."

### III.
He tripped on his own figs, cracked a bone,
The floor laughed, a maw of jagged stone.
"Give in to the urge," the walls whispered, slick with his blood,
"The void's your womb - the only god you'll clutch."

### IV.
So he stopped. Let the shadows stuff his throat with their wish,
Swallowed the stars that had eaten his limbs.
He chose wickedness over his own living,
The hallway pulsed, now a womb of his own making,
He was no longer aware of what he is becoming.

## Time Flew
### I.
Three winters gnawed his cheeks to ash,
Now ten, his laugh a crusted latch.
He codes in basements, cold and bright,
Fingers tap in binary night.
A soul stripped to a numbered spine,
His pulse? A glitch in the machine's design.

### II.
The screen's blue glow etches his eyes,
A map of stars he'd once kissed goodbye.
He writes algorithms to erase the *why?*,
To fold the void into if-then lies.
"*Efficiency,*" he chants, a hollow creed,
While his heart turns a defunct subroutine.

### III.
He carves his name in unchartered load,
Then erases vowels: lets the letters float.
"What's a name but a trap?" he smirks, half-dead,
As his reflection splinters, unread.
The walls hum "Goodbye" in ungodly manner,
His childhood's ghost is now an untraced lug.

### IV.
He thinks he is still at zero but the knowledge progresses,
Learning more each day, losing his own mind's way.
Stunning progress as impersonated people,
Afloat a mind in desperate and utter fear.
The final line of his previous code? A question to his soul:
"// What remains when you delete your own spark?
 // Is it the veins or your intimate underlined war."

## Impure Humanity
### I.
His mother's voice? A glitch in the static's hum,
"Eat, dear," she pleads: "but why feed a tomb?"
He counts calories like lines on a barcode,
A symphony of zeroes like his body will explode.
His mind says it is worth the hassle, but,
The mirror shows a hollowed cipher,
A machine that once was a child, now turned a sinner.

### II.
The walls whisper in haunting tongues,
"You're pure now: unstuck from the slings of young."
He nods, their logic flawless, divine,
As shadows claw at his spine, align,
To the rhythm of voices that "fix" his mind:
"Schism is clarity," the algorithm grinds.

### III.
He starves yet swells, a paradox of flesh,
A clockwork that dwells whose gut's ghost amends,
To feast on the void, "Efficiency," he sighs,
"The system's flow is the flesh that lies."
His limbs, looked like meat, reject the sun:
"Movement's a course that cannot be undone."

### IV.
The doctor scribbled some "anorexia" in red,
He laughs and quietly hid that under his old bed,
The flawed system's head said that he is being led.
He quietly whispered that he might go dead.
Hence he shone, all in the wilderness alone,
The crimsons where he began gave a bad tone,
He lied when he said "I am pure and could be brought back home."

### V.
"You missed the math," wall whispered,
To which he said "Go fuck yourself before I put a Winchester".
He think he grew gold in his yard but it was in turn his own flesh,
Couldn't have said it better when the wall said "You surely are going next."
"The weight I lose, the world grows cold."
His joints start crackling as appears weird mold.
"Oh hey I am decaying, could that be pine?"
No, that was the last hope hanging from a vine.
Too analog, too blind,
Unmarred by his thoughts, he couldn't be right.

### VI.
Since came pride, brought home a no-life,
He was rotting white while he thought he was shining bright.
Well, the mind's pure when it's voided,
God was slowly distancing from the equation.

### VII.
He codes his epitaph in encrypted script:
"// Delete the host. The ghost's the gift."
Unaware and blinded that the ghost could be evil,
The words seemingly tasted him like freedom.
"I'm more than human," he hisses, half-dead,
The audience faded as they shook their head.
I believe he should disappear honestly, but he was kind,
*Until he recollects, his soul and piece will never together be lined.*

A child's soul, now unwind and tight.
"A system that never needs to be fed" was his fight.

## Elegy For The Doubting Knife
### I.
Twelve years of sweat-slicked sheets, of choking truths held his groans,
This body's a tomb where his hunger grows.
He stares at the plate: *Is this food or proof?*
The fork trembles with a Macbeth's dagger in throes.
"What if the walls are my own cracked mind?"
He whispers, while fat and famine entwine.
A carcass both starved and swollen with lies,
Where every heartbeat punishes him through the night strangling the sky.

### II.
The voices now laugh: "You're still the same fool-
That steak's just a skull. That mind's a device of bile."
He claws at his throat, tries to unlearn his rule:
"To eat is to rot in this endless trial."
But memory's teeth sink deep in his spine:
That night in the closet, the belt and the red wine..
"Am I mad" he sobs while being unable to cry.
"Or am I finally just seen for who I can try?"
The room spins to Dante's Cacytus in a frozen pie.

### III.
He looks tempted to eat his own knuckles with tastes of copper,
"Why not devour myself? Less dust to distrust and worser."
The archaic night and the mirror showed jaws where his cheeks used to gleam:
"I'd chew my own shadow if it filled the dream."
Faustus weeps in the narrow tone:
"All knowledge is ash when you eat what's your own."
His tongue licks the air for his dear last breath.
"Am I really thinking right or should I be sacked?"
He whispered as his last thought sank.

### IV.
Charming sounds of a sterile man, who came to check up on my health,
He soon to horror found that I was malnutritioned and nearly pressed.
Immediately came a list of vitamin pills and an urgent note,
Hence turned off my inner glowing stove.

### V.
The clock's tongue is still. The void holds its breath.
Two questions now coil like vipers in death:
"Shall I unchain the boy lost in the snow?
 Or let the last ember of me gutter slow...
 ...to rot in peace where the worms know my name,
 A feast for the dark and no spark left to show.
 Am I really to be blamed for what was a horror's night,
 Or am I too far gone to utter my plight?"
Things he said were striking, so was the clock,
It started moving faster as he woke up.

## Charge Away For He Must Find You
### I.
Scraped the vomit from the titles and tried to pray,
Scraped the blood from the knuckles as he washed the tray.
God's teeth clock behind the door's thin wood:
"You ate the world. Now choke on what you should."
The Bible opens to John 3:16.
Gag on the ink, for you must be too late. Too late.

### II.
He forced the spoon in. Forced the bread to stay.
Forced a smile at dawn, watched it fray.
He steered away from his ungodly ways,
For he sensed chasing him someone superior from far away,
Not in good reason or attempt but rather to destroy,
Before that happened, he must rejoice.

### III.
"You're still the thief
 Who stole the light and called it grief."
Scraped his ribs raw and tried to bleed out the life.
The floor laughs: "Rot here. Let the worms begin."
And hence he thought that he must change.

### IV.
Scraped the lies from mirrors: "I'm not this thing!"
Scraped his name from walls that no raven will sing.
He kneels where shadows touch the holy ground:
"Take my eyes, for I'll walk without a sound!"
But the cross bled and the altar's cold.
"You're already hell," the silence told.
Scraping the stars from sky where no flight occurs.
Twelve years of poison could not be rinsed,
The priest's voice drowns: "Your soul's been minced."
He kneels in glass and begs the void: "Just end it
Before the flames come to claim what's sinned."

### V.
Then came silence.
Not hell's red roar or the voids,
A light like stone steps and loids.
No fire, Just water, cold as mercy's hand,
Washing the rot from where his sins once began.
The rope forgot to hang. The worms retreat.
"One breath left... and the dark did not complete."

## Filth Cleanse
### I.
God-, God-, God-, he chokes on the name,
Utters prayers with all he had while his face turned pale.
Hands scrubbed raw where the books and knowledge stained.
Too late, the mirror screams, too late.
He counts his bones: twelve- thirteen, fourtee-,
While hell's breath chants under his thoughts of sail.

### II.
He swallows bread, real bread,
But throat convulses, spits it back.
"Forgive me!" -a shredded note
Flung at the altars, black as smoke.
Sins swarm thick as flies on meat,
Each one a hit in God's own wheat.

### III.
He washes, scrubs, bleeds on the tile,
Sees his own reflections in the soap's swirl.
"I'll be good!" -a child's cracked trial,
But the water's thick with his own mile.
Too much blood in the holy water,
Too much he had rot so I taught her.

### IV.
The fire starts here, in his own gut,
Not brimstone but the truth he put,
"Take it back!" -a strangled shout,
As his skin peels like overcooked bait, foul,
This is how hell begins: not below,
But in the throat where your last lie grows.

### V.
He collapses in fear, not yet ash,
For he could be if he dodged this chance.
Eyes fixed where the light won't land-
An outlined breathing through the crash,
Not fire but unburning sand.
One breath left in the hollowed air:
"Is this where the damned get to give a... goodbye?"

## Answer The Whys (Interlude)
### I.
Child, the shadow breathes, no thunder, only dust,
You died where the clock's tongue did not hit what it must.
Not flesh but mind first rotted into rust.
That's why the gates grant you this fragile hope of trust.
Hell's gates demand a corpse that's wholly burned;
Yours is a ghost that drowned before it learned,
To choke on grace, now kneel on glass and write,
The sins you swallowed which you can't go past.

### II.
Repentance is the knife you hold to your throat,
Not God's. Each wound must bleed your own design.
Scrape truth from bare to bone till no delusions can be fined.
That crimson content? The frost on that windowpane?
Ask not for mercy. Earn the half-light's claim.
"Forgive the hands that taught me how to hate,
 How would I heal if I took back my pride and lost my weight?"
Well, the pride's gonna be gone soon, don't know about the weight.
For that must be your mistake, could have called out God's name.
That should at the very least be the price for stealing heaven's gate.

### III.
Now watch. The shadow parts into a breath and a sigh,
You presently stand where a seven year old feet once froze,
Beside the book that bled across your eyes.
Your small hands tremble, turning crimson rows into your mind.
You scream to stop him, but your voice is snow:
No touch, no sound, just watching the horror unfold and grow.
Why are you here? Why can't you tear the page?
*The hallway throat asks: "Who's the ghost.. or the cage?"*

## Purgatario
### I.
"The ghost," he rasps, "is the fear I fed-
 The cage, the fear I let grow instead."
 Each scar I carved with my own two hands,
 Each lie I drank like sacrament sands.
 The hallway's walls? My trembling mind,
 Where every truth I sought was blind."

### II.
"So I chased the dark with a scholar's greed-
 Swallowed the world's poison to kill the need.
 Read tombs for lullabies, bled ink for bread,
 Let knowledge rot in the marrow instead.
 The more I knew, the less I saw-
 A moth feeding on the light to feed the raw.
 Fear built the cage; fear was the key..
 Now even silence screams at me."

### III.
The hallway's throat hums, soft as a psalm:
"You've named the wound. Does healing still alarm?
 Hath thou bleed truth from the same cracked vein?
 Or... would you unmake what you hath done?"
A pause thick as grave-silt. The air turns thin.
"You know the lock. Will you step in?"

### IV.
"No, " he chokes, "I'd rather rot than mend,
 *One breath could shatter what I pretend.*
 To touch the light? To trust the ground?
 My bones are sutures, my voice is drowned.
 Fear and emotion are not a chain, it's the air I breathe,
 A feast of rot beneath my teeth.
 If I tried to heal... the void would see-
 I hopefully wish that God would know the best of me."

### V.
He collapses where the half-light crawls,
Watching his 7 year self turn crimson pages,
That small hand trembling, that book's black jaws..
"Why can't I scream? Why can't I rage?
 How would I gleam myself through this pain?"
The hallway whispers: "You chose the cage."
His final thought came from an ironic call,
"If heaven's a room where the clocks all chime..
Does God sweep the dust He made from the wall?" (Dante's Divine Comedy: Purgatario)

## The Harsh Route
### I.
Loop one: He kneels on glass begging "Forgive!" but the words,
Are ash in his throat, Macbeth's unsung dirge.
Loop two: He scrubs all his chants with his thumbs,
Yet the ink bleeds "rot" where "grace" should surge.
Loop three: He swallows bread-spits crimson snow-
Loop four: He starts hating his original submit to knowledge.
Loop five: Simply begging the void to set him free.
But the hallway laughs: "You're the ghost who can't cope.
 We aren't all good down here, why else would we be serving purgatory?"

### II.
Loop six: He stops, lets the glass cut deep.
"I am the rot. I am the cage. I am. Less Than Zero."
No plea but a gasp, no prayer but just a weep.
The dust on God's floor shivers, then calms.
The hallway's throat unclenches: "Child, step through.
 The rot you named? That's how the light gets in.
 Hell's not a place, it's the lie you grew.
 Acceptance is key, now that you did,
 Walk where your 7 year old feet begin."

### III.
He wakes clean. No shivers in the veins,
No static where the heartbeat used to fray.
The mirror shows a face, not cracked but stained,
With light and not rust. He eats without the ghost trail.
The screen's blue hum? That is there but with realization.
The scar? A thread of gold through hollowed sky,
Not gone, but gilded. Safety found, unestablished.

### IV.
Never again. The gutter's ghost still breathes
In his left palm: a tremor, thin as wire.
He grips the earth for his new seedlet breathes,
And whispers to the sun: "I will not tire."
The hallway's dust? Now soil beneath his feet.
But oh- he glances back where shadows crouch,
That book still bleeds in crimson, cold and sweet..
"God," he begs, "let this be wiped than be someone's weep."

## Wickedness (Interlude)
### I.
Free-. The word hung, soft as a lullaby-
"I smell something bad." "Yes you do".
No chains in the marrow, no ghosts in the bread.
The scar? Just a seam where the light slipped and went,
The gutter a shadow where hope was, instead.
Free. The hallway dissolved into dawn's gentle glee,
His palms warm with soil, his breath clean as me.
Free. The 7 year old's scream and dream? A bird's distant plee,
Could've been the seed.
Free. God's floor hadn't been reached, he held no dust,
Only the grace without the tree.

### II.
Oh, you ached for this, didn't you?
You begged the void to stitch his wound with gold.
Pathetic. The truth? He grew wicked-
Not cured. More rotten. More cold.
He licked the hellfire like a child's stolen sweet,
Swallowed the void where his soul could beat,
Repentance? A joke he carved in a tree.
The reader could not have hoped for this devastating tea.
Purgatory's door? They kicked him to hell.
Karma's a bitch for all they could sell.
He could've sworn we were behind when it was set free,
For all he could care, he reached his dream.
Hell isn't punishment. It's where he's let loose.
And you?
You thought he'd bloom?

He chose the rot.
So must you.
He tasted the void and got to begging more.

## Elegy For The Unraveling
### I.
I wish the hallway's throat would choke on its own lies,
That book of crimson pulp you swallowed, must it rot in the pyre.
I wish the air that taught you to starve your own eyes,
Would gag on the whiskey-laced psalms of your sire.
I wish the walls that grew teeth on your tongue to taste the silence,
Would crumble to dust where your blood's smell blended.
Oh, child, the world fed you hell as your inheritance..
But I beg you to let the void unmake what it bred.

### II.
You aren't special, the world does it like that,
I see you are still gnawing the marrow of "why?"
While hell's furnace sings lullabies in your bones.
Just remember that the readers are also as interrogative as you.
Your pulse? A noose. Your breath? A slow die.
You wear your sins like a crown of put together dye,
The light you crave? Just maggots of knowledge in the feast,
The affection you seek? A knife's cold, hollow deed.
You carved your name in the void's own beast,
I believe he should come to settle things with thee.
Now the darkness lives where your heartbeat's gone numb,
As devastating as it is, it had been building from the dawn.

### III.
So I say goodbye to the hallway's false hymn,
Goodbye to the book that bled through your sleds.
Goodbye to the shadow that was your twin,
These chains are currently yours to deal with.
Goodbye to the rot that you mistook for bread,
To the man who raised you, must he not be in debt.
I hope he's proud when you heal for he must have been the man-
Who gave you your first taste of wheat.
Goodbye, simply goodbye-
This isn't forever but hell's currently tied.

### IV.
I know the void won't spare you, it never does.
It'll feast on your screams till your soul's a dry husk.
But hell's a liar, so are we all.
However you must crack your own teeth trying to keep what you always had.
So simply wait until the abyss forgets the name you hath.
The archaic spirits you back where the dawn bled,
Not saved but unsolved till claimed land is freed to be fed.
As usual, *hell can't digest a ghost who refuses to be dead.*
*Stick out if you could chew the prison's metal and bread.*
And hence, the elegy stays ticking on the clock's tongue.
The unraveling must proceed when the time has ticked one.
